How TripEase Coastal works

We don't sign up businesses one by one. We work through each village's own tourism group (Pokdarwis) or village enterprise (BUMDes), so the community stays in charge.

  1. 01

    Local onboarding

    Pokdarwis or BUMDes identify and verify homestays, boat operators, cooks and craft makers before they are listed.

  2. 02

    Guide training

    Guides get basic English, hospitality and safety training through university partners, KKN and internship programmes.

  3. 03

    Online booking

    Travellers compare open or private trips, see exactly what is included, and pay online before they arrive.

  4. 04

    The trip

    A verified local guide leads every trip. Daily limits keep visitor numbers within what each site can handle.

  5. 05

    Impact reporting

    Travellers rate the trip, and the conservation share of every booking is reported back to the village and to you.

List your homestay, boat or guiding service

TripEase Coastal partners with village institutions, not individual businesses. Register through your Pokdarwis or BUMDes and they will verify your listing.

  • 70% of every booking goes to local operators
  • Free English and hospitality training for guides
  • Your village sets daily visitor limits
  • No upfront fee. The platform only earns a 10% commission
