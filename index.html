How TripEase Coastal works

Technology that strengthens local communities instead of replacing them.

  1. Local onboarding

    Pokdarwis or BUMDes verify homestays, boat operators, cooks and craft makers.

  2. Guide training

    Basic English, hospitality and safety training with university partners (KKN, internships).

  3. Online booking

    Compare open or private trips, see what's included and pay securely online.

  4. The trip

    A verified local guide leads every trip. Daily quotas protect each site.

  5. Impact reporting

    You rate the trip, and the conservation share is reported back to the village.
